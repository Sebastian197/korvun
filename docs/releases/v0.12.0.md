# Korvun v0.12.0 — Beta: identity, intent and authority

The second release of the Execution Trust Layer era (Etapa 2 of 10). Every
recorded action now knows WHO asked for it, under WHICH human intention,
and with HOW MUCH authority — and authority can only shrink: the
attenuation wall governs everyone, the operator included. The engine stays
invisible in the app — the product experience does not move one pixel —
but it ships its first operator tool: `korvun intent` and `korvun grant`.

## What identity means here

- **A principal is born from authenticated provenance, never from text.**
  The channel a request rode (its config-pinned transport credential)
  decides who is acting; a forged `sender` claiming the operator's own id
  still resolves to a channel peer, with the claim surviving strictly as
  evidence data. The console — the operator's own hands — is the only
  provenance that IS the operator.
- **Every action records its chain**: the acting principal (an agent brain
  answers to the operator), the intent it acted under, the authority that
  explains it, and per-attempt identity evidence — provider, credential
  KIND (never a secret: secrets are unrepresentable by construction),
  subject and a canonical claims digest — committed in the SAME
  transaction as the attempt. Together or nothing.
- **Intent contracts and authority grants are first-class**: purpose,
  operation and resource sets, budgets, validity windows and a fail-closed
  lifecycle (DRAFT → ACTIVE → EXPIRED/REVOKED), each with a deterministic
  digest over its TERMS — revoking closes a contract's life, it does not
  rewrite its identity.

## Authority can only shrink

The §14.3 attenuation validator is a pure function judged by an
independent oracle, property-tested across 400 seeded rounds and fuzzed in
the permanent quality gate: a delegated grant must be a subset of its
parent in EVERY dimension — operations, resources, budget, expiry,
validity window, strictly decreasing delegation depth, intent, parentage
and issuer chain. A widening child is rejected NAMING the dimension and
never touches the disk. The same wall guards the kernel's boot-derived
grants, the store's delegation frontier, and the operator's own CLI.

## The operator's act

`korvun intent create | activate | revoke | list | show` and
`korvun grant issue | delegate | revoke` work against the local store with
brief, WAL-safe access. Every mutation — including every refusal — leaves
an identified receipt: the operator as principal, loopback evidence, and a
finite audit rule (`operator` for the act, `attenuation_violated` for a
delegation the wall refused). The release was accepted through exactly
this ceremony, executed on the director's real profile: a limited intent
(«semana de pruebas», calc only, 10 actions, 7-day expiry) created and
activated, bounded authority issued, a deliberately widening delegation
dying at the wall with its dimension named — and four receipts with live
identity columns telling the whole story.

## New vocabulary (finite, adjudicated)

The decision-rule grammar grows only these labels: `intent_inactive`,
`intent_expired`, `authority_expired`, `authority_revoked`,
`authority_inactive`, `budget_exhausted`, `principal_disabled`,
`operator` and `attenuation_violated`. None of them is reachable by
today's flows under the root intent — they exist for the contracts the
operator writes.

## Budgets that hold

Budget consumption is serialized check-and-increment — under a
race-detector hammer of 4×N concurrent consumers exactly N are granted,
denials consume nothing — and durable: a restarted store remembers what
was spent, and an interrupted consume counts zero.

## Compatibility

**Zero new configuration, zero manual migration, zero experience change.**
The store's first real migration (v1→v2) runs automatically inside one
transaction per step — a crash mid-migration rolls back to a clean
previous version and the next boot completes it, proven by test at the
worst crash point. Existing rows stay readable with null identity; every
Etapa-1 receipt digest remains valid and comparable (the digest algorithm
is untouched, pinned by test). The whole pre-existing suite passes
unmodified, and the identified hot path measures ~0.8–1.1 ms per call —
still far under the 5 ms p95 ceiling.

## Verify your download

Both families ship a sha256 manifest signed with cosign (keyless,
Sigstore) and an SPDX SBOM per platform:

```sh
cosign verify-blob checksums.txt \
  --signature checksums.txt.sig \
  --certificate checksums.txt.pem \
  --certificate-identity-regexp 'https://github.com/Sebastian197/korvun/.*' \
  --certificate-oidc-issuer https://token.actions.githubusercontent.com
```

(Desktop family: the same command over `checksums-desktop.txt`.) Full
walkthrough: [`docs/packaging/INSTALL.md`](../packaging/INSTALL.md).
