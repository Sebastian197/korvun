# Korvun v0.13.0 — Beta: effects and per-action policy

The third release of the Execution Trust Layer era (Etapa 3 of 10).
Korvun now decides on the CONSEQUENCE of an action, not only on its
name: every operation carries a declared class on a consequence ladder
with a total order; effect ceilings wake as attenuation's tenth
dimension; every gate decision pins the exact law that took it; and
under bounded authority, the irreversible demands a human yes — which,
until stage 5 ships the approval workflow, dies with the honest no
`approval_unavailable`. The engine stays invisible in the app: today's
flows do not move one pixel.

## The consequence ladder

Every operation declares its Effect Descriptor in a validated registry —
never inferred from model text, never from parameters (the classifier's
input is the operation name alone, pinned by a trap test). The finite
classes carry a DECLARED total order:

`pure < read_external < write_reversible < write_compensatable <
write_irreversible < critical`

with a fail-closed law folded in: any unknown class ranks ABOVE
critical, so garbage can never sneak under a ceiling. The honest calls
for today's tools: `calc`/`time`/`echo` are pure; `read_file` and
`http_fetch` read external state with data egress; `memory_note` is
`write_reversible` (a note that persists IS a write — the ladder
measures consequence, not network distance); `webhook_call` is
`write_irreversible` (an arbitrary downstream POST has no documented
undo). An operation without a descriptor fails the boot preflight, and —
defense in depth — dies at the gate with `effect_undeclared`.

## The ceiling: attenuation's tenth dimension

Authority grants gain an `effect_ceiling`, comparable exactly because
the order is total. Delegation must shrink here too: a child's ceiling
must sit at or under its parent's on the ladder — with the
zero-is-unlimited trap covered (a limited parent with an absent child
ceiling is a widening) — judged by the same oracle-checked,
property-tested, fuzzed validator as the other nine dimensions, at the
store's frontier, on the operator's CLI (`--effect-ceiling` on issue
and delegate, with inheritance), and at the action gate (`effect_ceiling`
denials carrying the real refused class). The release was accepted
through the ceremony on the director's real profile: authority with a
`read_external` ceiling born, and a `critical`-ceilinged child dying at
the wall NAMING the dimension.

## Every decision pins its law

Gate decisions persist `policy_version` and `policy_digest` — the
brain's effective governance plus the effect-registry snapshot; a change
in either is a different law. The three blueprint-mandatory contracts
hold end to end: changed arguments force a new decision (never reuse); a
config reload NEVER alters recorded decisions (immutable rows — the old
row keeps its law, the next attempt pins the new one); and read vs write
receive different outcomes under ONE pinned law. The blueprint's exit
criterion stands: Korvun can explain WHY a specific action was allowed
or denied — who, under which intention, with what authority, what class,
which rule, which exact law — in one row.

## The honest no

Under BOUNDED authority (a ceilinged grant — never the root's standing
authority), `write_irreversible` and `critical` actions require human
approval. No approval workflow exists until stage 5, so they die DENIED
`approval_unavailable` — and anything demanding a prepare phase dies
`prepare_unavailable`. Nothing ever passes for lack of machinery. Stage
5 will turn those noes into approval cards.

## New vocabulary (finite, adjudicated)

`effect_undeclared`, `effect_ceiling`, `approval_unavailable`,
`prepare_unavailable` join the finite decision grammar. None of them is
reachable by today's flows under the root intent.

## Compatibility

**Zero new configuration, zero manual migration, zero experience
change.** Receipts are untouched BY CONSTRUCTION: the effect class is
row data, never a digest input — the same logical action yields the same
digest before and after the stage, pinned by test, with pre-E3 derived
grant ids captured as literals that must never move. The store migrates
v2→v3→v4 automatically, one transaction per step, crash-proof at the
worst abort point (proven against hand-built era files); historical rows
keep their honest `unclassified` class and unpinned law, never rewritten.
The whole pre-existing suite passes unmodified, and the classified hot
path measures ~0.9–1.7 ms per call — far under the 5 ms p95 ceiling.

## Verify your download

Both families ship a sha256 manifest signed with cosign (keyless,
Sigstore), an SPDX SBOM per platform — and, new in this release, **build
provenance as a release asset per family** (`*_provenance.intoto.jsonl`,
a Sigstore bundle wrapping the DSSE-signed in-toto SLSA statement over
each signed manifest):

```sh
cosign verify-blob checksums.txt \
  --signature checksums.txt.sig \
  --certificate checksums.txt.pem \
  --certificate-identity-regexp 'https://github.com/Sebastian197/korvun/.*' \
  --certificate-oidc-issuer https://token.actions.githubusercontent.com

gh attestation verify checksums.txt --repo Sebastian197/korvun
```

(Desktop family: the same commands over `checksums-desktop.txt`.) Full
walkthrough: [`docs/packaging/INSTALL.md`](../packaging/INSTALL.md) and
[`SECURITY.md`](../../SECURITY.md).
