## C2 — desktop SBOMs: recommend "the web stops promising them"

**Evidence.** `gh release view v0.15.0 --json assets`: six
`korvun_0.15.0_*.sbom.json`, all headless; zero desktop SBOMs.
`release-desktop.yml` has no SBOM step; `release.yml` installs Syft and
GoReleaser emits the six. Claims that promise a desktop SBOM, read on disk:
`SECURITY.md` (two families "each with an SPDX SBOM"), `README.md` ("a
per-artifact SBOM"; "Releases … ship an SBOM"), `website/docs/releases/index.md`
and its ES mirror ("plus the desktop apps: each artifact is covered by … and an
SBOM"). The landing copy, the install guides and `docs/packaging/INSTALL.md`
speak of the six headless archives and are true.

**Recommendation: stop promising, now.** Reasons: (1) cost — generating them
means a new step on three native runners, Syft over a `.dmg`, an NSIS installer
and a tarball, plus wiring the SBOMs into `checksums-desktop.txt`, its cosign
signature and the finalizer's presence asserts; (2) CI surface — `release.yml`'s
own comment records that the Syft download step pins the action but not the
Syft binary, so a copy of it into release-desktop inherits a declared exception
(pinned-action rule); (3) verification — a desktop SBOM is only evidenced by a
dry-run plus a tag; the text change is verifiable today against the published
assets. Generation can be a later train with its own design.

**Class sweep (pass-1 P2-2).** `CONTRIBUTING.md` ("SPDX SBOMs per platform"
for both families) was a fifth door; cured. The whole-tree grep is kept in
`evidence/v0151-c/c2-sbom-grep.txt`, regenerated by `capture.sh` with its command
and exit code (pass-3 P3-F) (`git grep -n -i sbom` excluding cantos,
stages, HANDOFF, specs, ADRs, older release notes, workflows, lockfiles and Go).
Every remaining hit either speaks of the six headless archives (landing copy,
install guides EN/ES, what-is EN/ES, `docs/packaging/INSTALL.md` which opens on
the headless binary), of the CI `sbom` job, or of the pipeline in general
(`MASTER.md`, roadmaps, the release checklist's v0.9.2 mold, the best-practices
questionnaire pointing at INSTALL.md); none promises a desktop SBOM.

**Change applied (the director's decision).** Eight sentences in seven files
(SECURITY.md 1, README.md 2, releases index EN 1 and ES 1, CONTRIBUTING.md 1,
what-is EN 1 and ES 1, the last two tightened by pass-2 P3-D) scoped to "the
