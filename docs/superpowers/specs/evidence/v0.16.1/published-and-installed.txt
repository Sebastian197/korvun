## 5-bis · What this install DID, and what nobody can account for

The gateway **started**. `(*App).Start` ran through: a brain named `default` was
wired to ollama `llama3.2:1b`, the admin server listened on `127.0.0.1:50454` —
loopback, as the invariant requires — no channel was configured, and the process
printed `korvun is serving`. The profile ends with `korvun.json`, `korvun.db` and
a receipt-signing key on disk.

So the onboarding was not merely opened, it was **completed**, 22 seconds after
launch. The capture
`docs/assets/captures/v0.16.1/installed-from-the-published-dmg.png` was taken
BEFORE that, and shows the first step — «Bienvenido a Korvun», the model question
— which is what a new user meets.

**And this record cannot say who completed it.** Between the capture and the
quit, nothing in this pass clicked anything: the only commands issued were a
frontmost-set, a `screencapture` and the `pkill`. Whether the app auto-advanced
because a local ollama answered, or something else drove it, is NOT established
here, and the honest state of that question is *unknown*, not *benign*. It is
filed rather than explained away.

**What is still NOT claimed.** No channel was configured and no approval was
decided from this install. And «no model was reached» is NOT asserted either way:
`model wired` is wiring, not a call, but `Start` also fires a best-effort warmup
against the local model, and this pass did not probe whether it landed.

What the install DOES establish: the published artifact installs by its
documented mold, is the bytes the signed list names, runs, reports its own
version, and reaches a serving state on a fresh profile. Everything else about
this release's behaviour rests on `manual-pass.txt` and `packaged-pass.txt`, at
the levels each of them declares.
