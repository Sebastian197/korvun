## Addendum — finding 1 of the twelfth external review (2026-09-05)

The in-tx idempotence read (`tombstoneStoredRowTx`) was the LAST
reader outside the one contract: it scanned `policy_version` straight
into an int64 and never called `judgeStoredTombstone`, so a
type-corrupt existing row surfaced from `tombstoneTx` as a naked
driver scan error (`errors.As(TombstoneFault)` false) and a
present-but-empty `decision_at` was disguised as `tombstone_conflict`.
Cured in this addendum's commit: the row is read AS RAW TEXT
(`CAST(policy_version AS TEXT)`) and judged by THE one contract before
the R10-V2 projection; the projection semantics (stored bytes on both
sides) are untouched. Callers enumerated by grep: `tombstoneTx` has
TWO direct production call sites (approvals.go, the decide path and
`closeApprovalTx`); `tombstoneStoredRowTx` has ONE (inside
`tombstoneTx`) and no test caller.

## Addendum — findings 2 and 3 of the twelfth external review (2026-09-05)

One rule, two doors. The origin rule only knew the literal clock verb
(finding 2: `CLOCK`, `expired`, `bogus` with a coherent principal and
digest passed every reader with err=nil), and the origin wall lived in
`tombstoneTx`'s callers, not in the write funnel (finding 3). Cured in
two commits, per the director's conditions:

1. `refactor(action)` — the decision verb vocabulary gained its SINGLE
   source: `DecisionApproved/Rejected/Cancelled/Clock` and the
   predicate `IsHumanDecision` in `internal/action/approval.go`, next
   to `ApprovalStatus`. It had NO single source before: literals in
   the switch of `decideApprovalWithLaw`, four loose comparisons in
   the same file, the two clock origins, the tombstone contract and
   the operator CLI. Zero behavior change; the grep of quoted verbs
   over production code outside `approval.go` returns nothing. The
   pin test freezes every constant byte for byte (they are digest
   terms of every stored row); its probing mutations — the predicate
   accepting anything, `DecisionClock` renamed — reddened both molds
   and were reverted by reverse edit, md5-verified.

   principal)` in `store.go` is THE rule: clock → empty principal;
   human verb (the predicate) → named principal; anything else →

   idempotence read). Write door: `tombstoneTx` BEFORE its INSERT, the
   typed fault through `%w`, nothing written (observed INSIDE the
   refusing transaction, not after the rollback). The S2-1 wall in
   `decideApprovalWithLaw` delegates to the same function; its
   literal switch died with it.
