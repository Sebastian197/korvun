# R15 — the tombstone judged whenever it is addressable

A train of the Action Kernel's verifier. Two changes of logic, two
corrections of message, and a paper that took nine versions and seven
adversarial passes to stop being wider than its wire.

## 1. What the train does

**The elevation.** `korvun receipt verify` judged the tombstone evidence
only when retention had already taken BOTH the approval row and the
action row: the reconstruction arms hung inside the both-rows-absent
branch. So a tombstone forged beside a LIVE approval was never read, and
the verifier printed OK and exited 0 over evidence anyone could see was
mutated. The lookup is now unconditional for every v2 receipt that seals
an approval digest, and the tombstone's verdict is appended AFTER the
approval's, which is the order the published ladder promises.

**The JOIN fail-open.** `Store.Get` is a JOIN of `actions` with
`action_decisions`. Its single `ErrNotFound` fires when the action row is
gone AND when only its decision row is, and the verifier read it as the
first. Two consequences, both live: it printed `action_row_absent`
claiming a retention prune over a LIVE action row, and it RETURNED on
that note, silently skipping the custody comparisons. Worse, the same
misreading disarmed the `approval_mismatch` arm: `DELETE FROM approvals`
failed as it should, and a second `DELETE FROM action_decisions` — a
child row, no foreign-key override — turned the whole verdict into exit
0 with two notes narrating a prune that never happened.

The two questions are now asked separately, through a read that only
asks them (`ActionRowsPresent`: one statement, two `EXISTS` sub-selects,
no rows materialised, no writes). Three named outcomes: both rows gone
is the cascade's own shape and gets the honest note; either one
surviving alone is `action_evidence_incomplete` and FAILS, because
retention removes them together; both present runs the normal path with
no premature return.

**Two messages corrected, by the director's message-not-logic ruling.**
The `action_row_absent` note stopped naming a cause it cannot observe.
And `ledger check`'s summary stopped saying "N receipt(s) with their
action row pruned by retention": it counts NOTES, not receipts; one
receipt can emit two; the notes it counts include the approval
reconstruction and the ambiguous-absence note; and after the JOIN finding
it cannot attribute any of them to retention.

## 2. Evidence

Level, everywhere: **in-process CLI suite over a real SQLite file, with
the mutations applied through a SEPARATE raw connection.** Not a compiled
binary in another OS process, and sold as nothing else.

**The first red, against base:**

**The JOIN cure's four molds**, in `internal/cli/receipt_r15_join_test.go`,
one per outcome plus the auditor's two-delete reproduction. Each asserts
the exact NAME and the exact TEXT — the text half closes a hole this
train found in itself: the wording of two notes was rewritten mid-train

The auditor's reproduction, after the cure:

```
exit 1 "FAIL approval_mismatch: … no approval row exists for act_inbox1
        while its action row remains — a cascade cannot do that
        FAIL action_evidence_incomplete: the action row for "act_inbox1"
        is present but its decision row is gone — retention removes both
        together, so this is evidence no cascade can produce"
```

**Three conforming mutations, executed, each discriminating:**

| Mutation | Neutralises | Reddens |
|---|---|---|
| `m-join-a` | the both-rows-gone arm | outcome (a) |
| `m-join-b` | the incomplete-evidence FAILURE, made a note | outcome (b) and the fail-open reproduction |
| `m-join-c` | restores the premature return | outcome (c) |

The source was copied aside before each and restored from that copy; the
final tree carries no mutation.

**And an inherited mold that was pinning an unreachable state.**
`TestReceiptVerify_prunedActionRowIsANamedNoteNotALie` opened its raw
connection without `foreign_keys(on)`, so its `DELETE FROM actions` left
an ORPHAN decision row — while the schema declares `ON DELETE CASCADE`
and the store's DSN enables foreign keys. It was asserting a shape
retention cannot produce, which is why it stayed green over the
fail-open. Its DSN now enables foreign keys and it counts the cascaded
row to prove its own premise. Its assertions are unchanged.

## 3. The paper, and what it cost

Nine versions, seven adversarial passes, every one returning VETO
MANTENIDO. The honest summary is that the GUARANTEE survived all seven
untouched; what failed, every single time, was the prose around it — the
letreros, the tables, the counts, the citations. Each pass pushed the
same defect one layer closer to the wire: states, then mechanisms, then
effects, then whether the mechanism was WIRED at all, then whether the
row the code calls "the action row" is one row.

The last of those is why this train has a second cure. The seventh pass
found that the JOIN does not only govern what is printed — it governs
whether a failure exists.
