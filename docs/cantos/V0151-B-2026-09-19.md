# V0151-B — the operator doors judge, name and seal what they can prove

Block B of v0.15.1. The approvals surface answers only a loopback peer; an
`invalidated` answer carries the law of the request's own brain; a failed
write inside a decide is never «already closed»; the screen paints an outcome
only for the exact success shape; an approval row that was read and does not
convert is corrupt evidence, one that could not be read is unreadable, never
both; every closing receipt that cannot use its approval row seals one mark
from a closed list, and both verifiers fail by that mark's class; an approval
id is judged by its minted shape at the operator doors and printed through the
escape alphabet; `-h` is a query on every operator verb.

The paper is `docs/superpowers/specs/2026-09-19-v0151-b-pretest.md`: thirteen
adversarial passes over the red phase, VETO LEVANTADO on the thirteenth.
The green step was ordered by the coordinator on 2026-09-19 on base
`origin/master` `e030202` (block A merged). Diff pass #1 held the veto on one
P2 [PRODUCT] (the sweep's first read and loop, P2-a) with four P3 (b, c, e
here; d routed to block C); their cures are in this tree, and a re-pass
scoped to that delta has not run yet.

## 1. What the train does

**P2-7 — the approvals surface and the console's message route answer a
loopback peer only.** The four approvals routes and the console's
`POST /api/conversations/{key}/message` refuse a peer whose TCP address is not
loopback with 403 `loopback_only`, BEFORE the bearer and before the seam
(`loopbackOnlyApprovals`, `internal/controlapi/peer.go`; `loopbackOnlyConsole`,
`console.go`). No opt-in. `127.0.0.1` and `::1` are served. What it judges is
the connection's peer address and nothing else: a same-host process that
forwards remote traffic over loopback (a reverse proxy, an SSH tunnel) is a
loopback peer and is served — named in the paper (§1, §9) and not claimed.
`loopback_only` joins the closed registry (21 → 22), with its approved
literal in §12-ter of the UX doc.

**P2-8 — `invalidated` carries the request's own brain's law.**
`currentLawDigest` resolved the law of `cfg.Brains[0]` whatever brain parked
the request. It now resolves it through `resolveLawFor` — the law of the
brain named by the request's own preview (`internal/app/approvals_adapter.go`).
An id that is not a request (the list door) carries no law.

**P2-9 — a failed write inside a decide is not «already closed».**
`transitionTx` names its two cases itself: a driver failure (the UPDATE, or
its `RowsAffected`) is `ErrApprovalWriteFailed` — new, declared in
`approvals.go`, the twelfth approval sentinel — and zero rows is
`ErrApprovalActionNotPending`. The approve branch passes the error through
without re-typing it (it used to stamp every failure NotPending); the reject
path and the expiry touch inherit the typing through `rejectParkedActionTx`.
The adapter names `ErrApprovalWriteFailed` `unavailable`; nothing was decided
and the transaction rolled back. `RowsAffected`'s error branch is defence in
depth, declared without a mould: modernc v1.58.0 does not fail it on a
completed statement.

**P2-10 — the screen judges the POST answer by protocol.** `judgeDecision`
(`Approvals.tsx`) paints an outcome only for a 200 whose body is exactly one
of the core's success shapes (`controlapi.ApprovalOutcome`): approve with
`outcome` `executed` or `failed`, reject with `rejected`; `receipt_id` a
string; `digest` and `result` absent or strings. Anything else — another 2xx,
the other verb's outcome, a field of the wrong type — paints the approved
«no reconocido» literal and affirms nothing. The five names a POST can carry
(`not_found`, `unavailable`, `disabled`, `params_digest_mismatch`,
`loopback_only`) are painted under their literals (v36 for the first three;
the 2026-09-19 approved literals for the last two), each with «Volver a
pendientes»; `params_digest_mismatch` also offers «Volver a leer la petición»,
which its literal tells the operator to do.

**P2-6 — one class per failed read of an approval row.** The row is scanned
into raw driver values (`scanRawApproval`, `internal/action/sqlite/approval_rows.go`),
which cannot fail on content, and converted in Go (`approvalFromRaw`), which
follows what `database/sql`'s typed Scan accepted before — with one declared
difference (§4). A value
that was read and does not convert — text in an integer column, a time that
does not parse, a NULL where the old destination was not nullable — is
`ErrApprovalEvidenceCorrupt`; every error Scan itself returns (a cancelled
context, a closed store, a statement that cannot be prepared) is
`ErrApprovalUnreadable`. Never both: the split is by construction, not by
error text (`classifyApprovalRead`). It holds on every door that reads the
row: `GetApproval`, `ListApprovals`, the decide (`approvalTx`), the sweep,
`ApprovalDetail` and `approvalTx0`; the decide's and the sweep's `BeginTx` are
unreadable too, and so are the sweep's first read (the SELECT that collects
the expired ids) and its loop's `ctx.Err()` — both wrapped with no sentinel
until diff pass #1 found them (P2-a), now `ErrApprovalUnreadable` with the
cause kept.

**P2-6 sealer — a closed list of marks, never a silent `""`.**
`approvalDigestTx` (`ledger.go`) reads the approval row raw with its `status`,
and a closing receipt that cannot use it seals exactly one of
`corrupt:row_scan`, `corrupt:decision_at`, `corrupt:decision_principal`
(`judgeTombstoneOrigin`'s rule: a human verb requires a principal, the clock
verb requires none), `corrupt:decision_verb`, `corrupt:approval_missing`,
`corrupt:status`, `unreadable:driver`; never `err.Error()` or cell bytes. On
all three sealing doors: the finish, the recovery and the reject path
(decide-reject, expiry touch, sweep). **The decided wire:** the closing door
hands the sealer a `decided` flag — `FinishWithResult` from the `current`
state it already read (APPROVED ⇒ decided); `closeCrashOrphan` from its
owning pass (`predicate == claimedOrphanPredicate`, proved by that UPDATE's
own RowsAffected == 1), with NO read before the UPDATE; the reject path
`true`. A missing or PENDING row is `approval_missing` only when `decided`;
a life with no approval keeps `""`. Not claimed, by design (R11): a coherent
rewrite of a decided row to another valid instant, principal and verb; a verb
rewritten coherent with the principal but not with the status (paper §1).

**P2-6 verifiers.** `receipt verify` and `ledger check` (both through
`verifyReceiptChecks`) judge the approval arm by the sealed value's shape
first: a well-formed `sha256:` digest takes the existing arm; a corrupt mark
fails `approval_evidence_corrupt`, an unreadable mark
`approval_evidence_unreadable`, anything else (a `sha256:` prefix of the
wrong shape included) `approval_evidence_unknown_mark` — and the rest of the
arm is skipped, so that receipt names exactly one approval failure, with the
row intact and after the cascade took it.

**P2-1 — the id's shape at the operator doors, and the escape alphabet.**
`action.ValidApprovalID` (`apr_` + 32 lowercase hex, what `NewApprovalID`
mints). The CLI's `show`, `approve`, `reject` and `execute` refuse a
malformed id with exit 2 BEFORE the config is loaded or any store is opened;
the refusal prints the id through the escape alphabet. The detail, approve
and reject routes answer `not_found` without calling the seam (the list
route takes no id). The screen lists a row with a malformed id under its
approved literal, not as a button; `RequestDetail` refuses to build a URL
from one. The CLI gained the screen's escape alphabet in Go (`escapeUntrusted`,
`internal/cli/escape.go`: Cc, Cf, Cs, every separator but the ASCII space,
Default_Ignorable_Code_Point, and `<` itself), applied to every stored field
`show` prints (the raw parameters included — the HANDOFF sister; the status
and the action digest since diff pass #1, P3-b) and to the id and risk summary
`list` prints. `list`'s status is not escaped and cannot carry foreign bytes:
it asks for the five known statuses one by one. `show`'s digest escape is
moulded by a coherent rewrite of the preview (the belt compares values, not
shape): `TestV0151B_P2_1_sister_showEscapesACoherentlyRewrittenDigest`, m68.
(The delta re-pass found that this paragraph first said the digest could not
be reached; that was false and is deleted.) The CLI's error lines on stderr
are not escaped (the entry «Found while moulding P3-b» in §4 and
`green/finding-show-stderr-raw-digest.txt`), and `show`'s comment now says so. The screen now escapes the row id, the
`korvun approvals show <id>` line and the receipt id. **Scope:** the adapter
and the store accept any string; nothing here claims they refuse by shape.
`{"approval_id":…}` in both operator envelopes (`cli/approvals.go`,
`approvalsOperator`) is built by `encoding/json` — declared without a mould.

**P2-2 — `-h` is a query.** Every verb of the `approvals`, `ledger`,
`receipt`, `intent` and `grant` families parses through `parseStyled`
(ADR-0032): `-h`, `-help` and `--help` answer exit 0 with the usage on stdout
and stderr empty; each noun answers `-h`/`--help` with its own usage. As
`parseStyled` does everywhere, the verbs also accept `--plain` and
`--no-color` (declared in §7 of the paper).

**X1 and the claim (director: in B's train).** `ClaimApprovalParamsUnderDigest`
propagates `approvalTx`'s error as it comes — one class — instead of wrapping
every non-absent error as corrupt; `ApprovalDetail` and `approvalTx0` classify
through `classifyApprovalRead`. These edits are in `approvals_v15.go`, block
A's file, declared in §7/§10 of the paper.

**The `already_decided` refusal claims nothing about executions.** Its

occurrences in controlapi, the CLI, the adapter and the screen were checked:

code, not a count of executions.

**Comments made true by this train:** `run.Unknown`'s comment in
`ApprovalsAdapter.runApproved` («every delivered request whose answer was

## 4. Declared without a mould, instrument defects, and what escaped

- `transitionTx`'s and the claim's `RowsAffected` error branches: modernc
  v1.58.0 never fails `RowsAffected` on a completed statement; defence in
  depth (paper §7).
- `encoding/json` for `{"approval_id":…}` in both operator envelopes: their
  callers (the CLI's approve and reject; the control API's approve and reject
  routes, before the adapter) refuse a malformed id by shape first, so no
  mould can reach a string that concatenation would have broken (paper §7).
  The adapter itself accepts any string.
- The P2-6 precheck/decide half at the wire: the window cannot be placed
  deterministically (paper §7).
- An integral REAL (3.0) in an integer approval column: the typed Scan took
  it, `approvalRawInt` names it corrupt. INTEGER affinity stores such a value
  as an integer, so it arrives only through a column rebuilt without that
  affinity. Declared in `approval_rows.go`, not moulded.
- The screen's `RequestDetail` shape guard (no URL from a malformed id): the
  list never opens such a row, so no mould reaches it; m58 watches the list.
- **m09 escaped, and that was the finding (doctrine, point 4).** The
  paper's «type only parse errors», applied to the wrong-KIND branches of
  `approvalRawText` / `approvalRawInt`, first ran green: no mould stored a
  value of the wrong storage class. On the coordinator's decision the mould
  was added: `TestV0151B_P2_6_aWrongKindCellIsEvidenceCorrupt`
  (`internal/action/sqlite/v0151b_wrongkind_test.go`) writes a REAL 3.5 and
  a BLOB x'00ff' into `approvals.policy_version` through a second connection
