## 6 · The cure that did not ship

The third ficha, «`ledger check` falla el recibo de la vida ANTERIOR de un id
reutilizado», was cured and then **reverted whole**, the same day, on the
adversary's P1.

| | |
|---|---|
| What it did | A receipt cannot begin before its own action was requested, so a row requested later is another life; the verifier then skipped the comparisons |
| Why it is wrong | It compared `receipt.started_at`, **sealed by the chain**, against `actions.requested_at`, which the chain does **not** cover and anything can write |
| The capture | `UPDATE actions SET requested_at='2099-01-01T00:00:00Z'` made a row whose `parameters_digest` no longer matched its seal answer `exit 0`. The same row gave `exit 1 custody_mismatch` at `32701e0`. A regression that lets a real tamper pass |
| The class it belongs to | «valid signatures over invalid or stale surrounding data»; Rule 2(b) inverted |
| What was kept | The ficha, re-filed with all of this, plus what the work learned: the class has TWO arms, not one, and the next cure must rest on something the chain covers or must only RE-LABEL a failure, never suppress one |

Reverting cost two cures' worth of work and left the tree exactly at master's
behaviour for that door. That is the correct trade and it is recorded here
rather than quietly dropped.
