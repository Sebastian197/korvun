## 5 · The ficha that was NOT cured, by adjudication

«Una clase de efecto futura se leería corrupta» stays as it is. The director
ruled on 2026-09-23 that the cheap cure is a downgrade and filed the real one to
piece 4 as «sealing the `effect_class` cell».

The reason, from what this branch verified in the tree: accepting a well-formed
unknown class costs no execution safety — unknown ranks 6, above critical, and
the adapter refuses to execute above the ceiling — but the conflicting row has
the preview AGREEING with the cell, and that case is caught by neither
`preview_effect_mismatch` nor the ceiling as a NAMED finding. The cheap cure
would turn a named finding into a **silent denial**. Sealing the cell catches
both a future class and a rewrite, without choosing.
